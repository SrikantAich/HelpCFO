      Common questions

      Who is HelpCFO for?

      Businesses that want dependable compliance, clean books or finance leadership without building a large in-house team. That includes startups, MSMEs, family-run businesses and established companies, along with trusts, societies and Section 8 companies.

      Which accounting software do you work with?

      We work in Tally, Zoho Books, QuickBooks, SAP or any ERP of your choice. We can also help you choose, implement or migrate to a new system.

      Can you handle notices from the GST or Income Tax department?

      Yes. We prepare replies to notices and show-cause notices, support assessments and audits, file appeals, and follow up on rectifications and refunds.

      What does a virtual CFO actually do?

      A virtual CFO gives you senior finance leadership on a part-time basis: management reports and dashboards, cash flow forecasts, budgets, working capital planning, and board, lender and investor reporting.

      Can you help us raise funds or arrange a bank loan?

      Yes. We assess your funding need, prepare CMA data and project reports, and support bank finance, government schemes, NBFC funding and investor-ready material for equity raises.

      What if the service I need isn't listed?

      Tell us what you need. We tailor our services to your business, and we will say plainly if something is outside what we do.
